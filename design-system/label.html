Label

Renders an accessible label associated with controls.

Basic

<label for="email-basic" class="text-sm font-medium leading-none peer-disabled:cursor-not-allowed peer-disabled:opacity-70">
  Your email address
</label>

With Input

<div class="grid w-full max-w-sm items-center gap-3">
  <label for="email-1" class="text-sm font-medium leading-none peer-disabled:cursor-not-allowed peer-disabled:opacity-70">
    Email
  </label>
  <input 
    type="email" 
    id="email-1" 
    placeholder="m@example.com" 
    class="flex h-9 w-full rounded-md border border-input bg-transparent px-3 py-1 text-sm shadow-xs placeholder:text-muted-foreground focus-visible:outline-none focus-visible:ring-1 focus-visible:ring-ring"
  />
</div>

With Checkbox

<div class="flex items-center gap-3">
  <button
    type="button"
    role="checkbox"
    aria-checked="false"
    data-state="unchecked"
    id="terms-label"
    class="peer size-4 shrink-0 rounded-[4px] border border-input shadow-xs"
  >
    <!-- Checkbox SVG -->
  </button>
  <label
    for="terms-label"
    class="text-sm leading-none font-medium select-none peer-disabled:cursor-not-allowed peer-disabled:opacity-50"
  >
    Accept terms and conditions
  </label>
</div>
